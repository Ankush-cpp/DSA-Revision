| 3982   | Sum of Integers with Maximum Digit Range | Easy       | Math + Digit Processing |

---

## Approach

* Traverse every number in the array.
* Find the maximum and minimum digit of each number.
* Compute the digit range (`maxDigit - minDigit`).
* Keep track of the maximum digit range found so far.
* Sum all numbers having that maximum digit range.

---

## Complexity

* **Time:** O(n × d), where `d` is the number of digits.
* **Space:** O(1)
